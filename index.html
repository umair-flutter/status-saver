01 Scope of this policy

This policy covers the Status Saver: Photo & Video Android app ("the app"), published under package name com.umairkhalid.statussaver. It explains what the app accesses on your device, what it sends to third-party services, and the choices you have.

The app is a utility for viewing and saving WhatsApp status media that WhatsApp already stores on your own device. Status Saver is an independent app and is not produced, endorsed, or affiliated with WhatsApp Inc. or Meta Platforms, Inc.

02 Media & storage access

To show you the photos and videos currently in WhatsApp's status folder, the app reads media files stored on your device. When you tap "Save," the app copies that file to a folder in your device's own Pictures library so it survives after the original status expires.

We do not upload, transmit, or store copies of your photos or videos on any server we control. All status media stays on your device unless you personally choose to share a file using your phone's share menu.

03 Permissions we request

Photos & videos

To find and display WhatsApp status media, and to save the ones you choose.

Notifications

To alert you when a new status becomes available, if you leave this on.

Internet

Required by the ad, analytics, and crash-reporting SDKs described below.

Run in background

Used briefly to watch for new status files and post the notification above.

05 Advertising & your choices

The app shows ads through Google AdMob to support free use of the app. AdMob may use your advertising ID and general device information to show ads relevant to you.

You can limit ad personalization at any time:

  • Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID or Opt out of ads personalization
  • Purchase "Remove Ads" inside the app to stop ads entirely

If you're in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom, the app will ask for your consent before showing personalized ads, in line with applicable law.

06 Analytics & crash reports

The app uses Firebase Analytics to understand aggregate usage (for example, which screens are opened) and Firebase Crashlytics to automatically report crashes so we can fix bugs. These reports may include device model, OS version, app version, and a stack trace of the crash — they do not include your status photos or videos.

07 In-app purchases

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not see or store your payment details — Google handles billing and shares only the purchase status with the app.

08 Notifications

If you grant notification permission, the app can alert you when new status media appears. You can disable this at any time in Android Settings → Apps → Status Saver → Notifications.

09 Data retention & security

Status media you save stays on your device under your control until you delete it. Analytics and crash data are retained by Firebase according to Google's standard retention periods and are not sold to third parties.

10 Children's privacy

Status Saver is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we'll remove it.

11 Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete data associated with you, and to object to certain processing. Because the app doesn't require an account or collect personal profile data directly, most of this data lives with our third-party providers (Google, Meta, Unity) — you can exercise these rights directly with them, or contact us and we'll help route the request.

12 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective" date at the top of this page.
